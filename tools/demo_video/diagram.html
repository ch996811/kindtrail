How a gift moves through Kindtrail

PayPal sandbox and Gemini on one side, Kindtrail's ledger and rule engine in the middle, three people using it.

  1. 1Donorabroad, gives in USDGives through PayPal Checkout. The server creates the order with the fund's code in custom_id.Orders v2 · JS SDK v6
  2. 2PayPalto KindtrailThe capture arrives by webhook. Its signature is checked before the gift enters the ledger, in integer cents.Webhooks
    verify-webhook-signature
  3. 3Field partnerin HualienUploads a receipt photo. Gemini returns each line: quantity, price, spending category and its box on the photo.Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite
    Gemma 4 as fallback
  4. 4Rule enginecode, not the modelChecks every line: category, region, date, a receipt sent twice, currency, totals. Anything it can't confirm isn't paid.review.py · 58 tests
  5. 5Reviewerat the charitySees each verdict and its reason. Approve allocates gifts oldest first and pays the partner; Send back pays nothing.Payouts
    sender_item_id = request
  6. 6PayPalto KindtrailThe payout is confirmed by a signature-verified webhook, and the request is marked paid.PAYMENT.PAYOUTS-ITEM
    .SUCCEEDED
  7. 7Donorgets a receiptLists the lines their gift paid for, the line the rules refused and the payout's PayPal transaction ID.Transaction Search
    reconciles the ledger
